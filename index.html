About it

Kahnaroc is a minimalist conlang designed as a lighter and simpler alternative to English with unique punctuation and constant pronunciation. Anything you can mean in English, you can in Kahnaroc.

I made this to simplify tenses and pronunciation. I also wanted to learn a new language without doing it the boring way, what better way is there than create your own? The language sounds barbaric as I tried to combine English and German together. I also wanted to experiment by creating an alphabet subset.

What really sets it apart from other languages, in my opinion, is the optional alphabet subset Kahnur, as well as English embedding and conversion.

Online Kahnur keyboard can be found here.

Brief Grammar Overview

Kahnaroc only contains 3 tenses - past, present and future. All nouns are non gendered and countable. Pronouns do not change verb suffixes. Kahnaroc uses unique punctuation. It includes an optional alphabet subset based off of Georgian Mkhedruli script.
